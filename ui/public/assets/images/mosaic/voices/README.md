# Discover review portraits

Created for Discover on 2026-09-07. All five subjects are fictional adults. The portraits are decorative UI photography: they do not identify the authors of the synthetic reviews or supply retrieval evidence. The existing review excerpts, ratings, purchase flags, and product links continue to come from Aurora.

The UI displays “AI-generated images are for illustrative purposes only. Reviews are from the synthetic catalog.” beside the strip. Each square PNG was resized and encoded once with `cwebp -resize 256 256 -q 85 -m 6`. The 256 × 256 WebPs below are the final runtime assets.

## Final assets and exact prompts

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: square photographic portrait for a small circular avatar on Mosaic, a premium ivory-and-maroon shopping demo. Warm natural window light, soft out-of-focus cream interior, casual everyday clothing, subtle genuine smile, direct relaxed gaze, candid editorial photography with realistic skin texture and small imperfections. Quiet, warm and approachable, not a corporate headshot or beauty campaign. 85mm portrait perspective, eye-level camera. No text, logos, borders, watermarks, products, accessories obscuring the face, or other people. Square composition.
