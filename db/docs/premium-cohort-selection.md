The included 120-product assignment is grounded in the physical 500K Mosaic catalog.

## Selection constraints

- exact target distribution: 48 electronics, 36 running/fitness, 36 home office
- all 27 pre-existing curated demo products retained
- six flagships mapped to the approved Mosaic product identities
- in-stock, non-sponsored, non-refurbished products preferred for added rows
- high metadata completeness required
- canonical groups deduplicated
- subcategory diversity favored before adding extra products from the same subcategory
- 30 retrieval anchors balanced at 10 per domain
