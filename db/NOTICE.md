# Notice

The served workshop catalog (`reviews-2023-v2`) is derived from Amazon
Reviews 2023 (McAuley Lab, UC San Diego); ESCI and WANDS labels appear only in
reviewed teaching comparisons. Sources, citations and license status are in the
repository [NOTICE](../NOTICE.md). The historical base catalog in this schema is
synthetic: its product identities, brands, reviews, offers, evidence and
performance data are invented. Premium imagery for that synthetic catalog should
use original industrial designs and must not reproduce third-party logos, trade
dress, or distinctive product-family features.

The schema uses 1024-dimensional vectors for the pinned Cohere Embed v4
workshop catalog. A different model or dimension requires a separately rendered
schema, regenerated embeddings, and fresh retrieval evaluation.
