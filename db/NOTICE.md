# Notice

All product identities, brands, reviews, offers, evidence, and performance data used by Mosaic are synthetic. Premium imagery should use original industrial designs and must not reproduce third-party logos, trade dress, or distinctive product-family features.

The schema uses 1024-dimensional vectors for the pinned Cohere Embed v4
workshop catalog. A different model or dimension requires a separately rendered
schema, regenerated embeddings, and fresh retrieval evaluation.
