Orion's GA slipped two weeks — July 1 → July 15 — because a P1 replication-lag blocker failed the release-readiness gate. The team decided the slip in Slack on June 23, and one contractual customer commitment (Acme Corp) is being renegotiated.

Why it's delayed. The events pipeline developed cross-region replication lag of up to 94 seconds against a 15-second freshness SLO, filed as P1 ORION-1473 on June 12 2. The Release Readiness Runbook's Gate 3 (data freshness) formally failed on June 18, and policy requires the GA date to slip until the gate passes 4. The same root cause triggered Sev2 incident INC-0012345 in production two days later 5.

What the team decided. After the readiness review, engineering lead Priya Mehta recorded the decision in #proj-orion on June 23: GA moves from July 1 to July 15, with partitioned WAL shipping as the hotfix path and CS notifying Acme the same day 1. The fix — PR #1287 — merged July 2 and cut lag p99 from 94s to 8s 6; the gate re-run passed on July 3, so July 15 is holding 2.

The decision, verbatim
“We're making the call: Orion GA moves from July 1 to July 15. ORION-1473 is the sole gating item — hotfix path is partitioned WAL shipping. CS to notify Acme before EOD.”
Priya Mehta · #proj-orion · Jun 23, 2026 · 4:12 PM · cited as [1]

Which commitments are impacted. One contractual commitment is directly affected: Acme Corp's go-live, promised for July 8 under an MSA addendum 3. The CSM is renegotiating to July 22 with a success-plan credit; the $1.2M renewal is flagged but the exec sponsor is engaged. No other Tier-1 commitments reference the Orion GA date in the current window 3.
