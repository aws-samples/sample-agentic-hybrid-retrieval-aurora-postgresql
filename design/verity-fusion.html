Why naive drifts

ts_rank here spans 0.023 – 0.408 (÷ max ⇒ dynamic range 0.94) while cosine similarity clusters at 0.649 – 0.792 (range 0.14). Feed both into a weighted sum and a 2 : 1 weight becomes roughly 13 : 1 ordering influence. Ranks are already commensurable — so Verity fuses ranks, and raw arm values persist only as diagnostics. They are never added.

The receipt keeps them apart

proof.retrieval_candidates stores arm positions, raw diagnostics, the Aurora RRF score, and the model rerank score separately — plus whether rerank ran, its timing, and its model ID. Rerank may reorder the pool; it never overwrites Aurora ordering. No score is presented as a probability.
