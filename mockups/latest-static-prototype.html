Why is Project Orion delayed and what customer commitments are impacted?
AM
Agent answer · 18 sources · 3 retrieval passes · 2.4 s

Orion is delayed by read replica lag at cutover — and two customer commitments are exposed.

Project Orion is delayed primarily due to read replica lag during the Blue/Green deployment cutover,[1] which prevented the cutover from completing inside the May 2 maintenance window. High database latency during the same period triggered a multi-service incident[2] and delayed customer-facing reporting for Acme Corp and Northwind Traders, both of which carry month-end commitments.[3] Engineering merged connection pool and failover improvements on May 3,[4] and the next cutover window is targeted for May 6 pending a lag soak test.[5]

Key points

Read replica lag spiked during peak load and blocked cutover to protect data consistency.[1]
High-latency incident INC0012345 impacted multiple downstream services on May 2.[2]
Acme Corp escalated reporting delays affecting monthly close.[3]
PR #1287 improves pool rebalancing and replica promotion time; merged May 3.[4]
