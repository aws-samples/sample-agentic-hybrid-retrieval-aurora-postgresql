## Cross-domain ambiguity

- `lightweight support for long sessions` — could refer to shoes, chairs, or wearables
- `quiet setup for travel` — could refer to headphones, compact fitness gear, or a mobile workspace
- `best under $200 with long battery` — domain intent must be inferred or clarified

Use ambiguous examples to teach query understanding and domain routing rather than pretending retrieval should guess silently.
